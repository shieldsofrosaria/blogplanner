# SL Blog Planner
I want to build another web app for planning & organizing my Second Life blog posts to easily export credits needed to submit them.
* * *
## Blogging Workflow
- Plan: Think of an idea for a post, including the avatar(s) to be used and the scene.

    - I want to get to the point to where planning ends up with a 80% or higher complete concept. If I play some things by ear (like I will rearrange decor while shooting) that's ok. But I don't want to walk into the next step with a vague idea.

    

- Build: Put together any sets or builds needed to complete the shot

    - I tend to get really critical of myself here, wanting the scene to be as fun and impactful as possible. I really like my realistic scenes to feel filled in/real. I like my more fun/cartoony/action scenes to feel dynamic and bold. Those are probably my two most used approaches. I want this to take a lot less time.

    

- Shoot: Take the or video\~

    - I also get hung up here when it comes to taking a 'good shot'. As much as I like photography/videography I think I need to immerse myself in more real world examples too. Maybe that will give me more insight & then it wont take as long to shoot? IDK, open to suggestions LOL

    

- Edit: Edit the image

    - My editing process will depend on the way I shoot and the end result I want. Lately I've either used GShade or shoot natively with SL's viewer (I use Firestorm). Afterwards I color correct it in the app Darktable. If its something I need to do more editing for (like add effects, remove mistakes) I will use Photopea. Usually I save a psd to preserve the edit & export as PNG.

    

- Share: Post blog photo & credits.

    - I want to be able to enter my own credits but I do have certain requirements per platform I share to:

        - Flickr: HTML friendly. No character limit AFAIK.

        - BlueSky: 300 character limit.

            - Required Hashtags: #SecondLife #SLFurry

            

        - YouTube (vlogs only):

            - I don't remember but you can probably see what would make for a good YouTube description? Because I have no idea LOL

            

        - Primfeed/Prim Network: Second Life specific platforms. I think rich text is okay, not sure about HTML. No character limit AFAIK.

        - Personal Blog (hosted on Tumblr): HTML is ok. No character limit AFAIK.

        

    


Notes: If there's any suggestions to overall optimize this workflow, I'm all ears! These are just the steps I need to take for each blog I do.
